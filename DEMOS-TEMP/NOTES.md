I've built a complete mobile-first PWA for **DropTeen** - a dropshipping academy for teenagers. Here's what's included:

## 🎨 **Design Features**
- **Dark mode** with auto-detection + glassmorphism effects
- **Floating animated background orbs** with gradient colors
- **Shimmer effects** on progress bars
- **Slide-up animations** for cards, modals, and messages
- **Scale animations** on tap (haptic-like feedback)
- **Instagram-style bottom nav** with active indicator
- **iOS-style bottom sheet modals** with backdrop blur

## 📱 **Pages & Navigation**
1. **Home** - Welcome card, XP progress, quick actions, daily tutorials, AI mentor list
2. **Steps** - 8-step roadmap with timeline (Mindset → Niche → Products → Store → Marketing → First Sale → Scaling → Brand)
3. **Products** - Search, filters, winning product cards with margins
4. **AI Chat** - Switch between 4 role-based expert mentors
5. **Profile** - Stats, achievements, business dashboard, settings

## 🤖 **AI Mentors (Role-Based)**
- 🧠 **Zara** - Business & strategy expert
- 💎 **Max** - Product hunting specialist  
- 🎨 **Luna** - Creative & branding pro
- ⚙️ **Tech** - Store building & automation

## 🛠️ **Tools Included**
- AI Business Coach
- Product Scraper (TikTok, Shopify, Ad Library)
- AI Store Builder
- Marketing Suite (TikTok, IG, Meta Ads, Influencer Hub)
- Analytics Dashboard
- Email, Retargeting, Inventory, Support Bot

## ✨ **Interactive Features**
- Working chat with contextual AI responses per mentor
- Quick-action suggestions
- Toast notifications
- Confetti animation on milestones
- XP/leveling system with progress
- Pull-to-refresh ready
- PWA install ready with theme colors

The app is fully self-contained, mobile-optimized, and uses pure HTML/CSS/JS with no dependencies.
